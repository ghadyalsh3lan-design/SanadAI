# IBTech — Training & Capacity Building Capability Statement

## Overview

As a national company aligned with Saudi Vision 2030, IBTech invests in
developing local digital talent. Our training and capacity-building practice
delivers structured, hands-on programs that prepare university students and
client staff for modern technology roles, and embeds knowledge transfer into
every managed-services engagement.

## Core Competencies

- **Technical training programs** — instructor-led and blended courses in
  software engineering, data, cloud, and artificial intelligence.
- **University capacity building** — engaging training programs for university
  students to prepare them for the digital era, supporting national workforce
  localization.
- **Knowledge transfer** — structured handover, standard operating procedure
  (SOP) documentation, and shadowing models built into service transitions so
  client teams can sustain operations.
- **Capability uplift for client staff** — role-based upskilling tied to the
  systems IBTech operates on the client's behalf.

## Program Areas

- **AI Engineering** — applied machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation,
  and MLOps fundamentals.
- **Data Engineering & Analytics** — pipelines, SQL, and business intelligence.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** — OCI and Azure fundamentals, virtualization, and
  operations.
- **IT Service Management** — ITIL-aligned operations and governance practices.

## Delivery Model

Programs combine classroom or virtual instruction with project-based, hands-on
labs and mentorship. Each engagement includes defined learning outcomes,
assessment, and a completion report. Knowledge-transfer activities within
managed-services contracts include documented SOPs, a maintained knowledge base,
and a phased Shadow → Reverse Shadow → Full Ownership handover.

## Standards and Governance

Training engagements are planned and tracked under PMI-based project governance,
with measurable learning outcomes and post-program evaluation feeding continuous
improvement.
