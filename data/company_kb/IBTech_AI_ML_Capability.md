# IBTech — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Capability Statement

## Overview

Intelligent Business for Information Technology (IBTech) operates a dedicated
AI & Machine Learning practice that designs, builds, and operates production
machine learning systems for government and enterprise clients in the Kingdom
of Saudi Arabia. Our work spans the full lifecycle — problem framing, data
preparation, model development, deployment, and ongoing MLOps — with an emphasis
on solutions that are explainable, auditable, and compliant with national data
protection requirements.

## Core Competencies

- **Predictive analytics and forecasting** — demand, capacity, and incident
  forecasting models using gradient-boosted trees and time-series methods.
- **Natural language processing** — Arabic and English text classification,
  named-entity extraction, and document understanding.
- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** — grounded question-answering and
  knowledge assistants built with LangChain, vector databases, and open-weight
  large language models, with mandatory source citation and refusal on weak
  evidence.
- **Computer vision** — object detection and image classification for
  monitoring and inspection use cases.
- **MLOps / LLMOps** — reproducible training pipelines, model registry and
  versioning with MLflow, automated evaluation, and monitoring of live models
  for drift and quality.

## Representative Engagements

- **Ministry of Interior — Unified Operations Centers (911), Riyadh, Dammam,
  and Madinah.** Delivered a machine learning incident-classification and
  triage component that routes incoming reports by type and priority, reducing
  manual dispatch effort. Built supporting predictive dashboards for operational
  load.
- **NEOM (Public Investment Fund).** Delivered a computer-vision proof of
  concept for automated monitoring as part of a digital integration program.
- **Internal RAG knowledge platform.** Built a retrieval-augmented assistant
  over the firm's past proposals and capability documents to accelerate bid
  response — the same architecture pattern offered to clients.

## Technology Stack

Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Sentence Transformers,
LangChain, Chroma, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker. Models are deployed on Oracle Cloud
Infrastructure (OCI) and Microsoft Azure depending on client environment.

## Standards and Governance

All AI engagements follow a documented evaluation protocol (faithfulness,
completeness, and task-specific accuracy), maintain human-in-the-loop review for
decision-support outputs, and align with PMI-based project governance and the
firm's ITIL-aligned operations model.
