# IBTech — Company Overview & Sector Experience

## Who We Are

Intelligent Business for Information Technology (IBTech) is a Saudi company
founded in 2019 and headquartered in Riyadh. IBTech is a branch of Al Baraah
Telecommunications (RBTech), established in 1997, and draws on RBTech's heritage
in wired and wireless communications and infrastructure. IBTech was launched to
support the growth of information technology, communications, and digital
business in the Kingdom, in line with Saudi Vision 2030.

The firm's executive team brings more than seven years of combined experience
delivering and operating technology projects for a wide range of entities. IBTech
runs a company-wide Project Management Office (PMO) aligned with PMI standards and
operates services using an ITIL-aligned IT service-management model.

## Service Lines

- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** — predictive analytics, NLP,
  computer vision, and retrieval-augmented generation.
- **Data Engineering & Analytics** — integration platforms, API gateways, data
  pipelines, and business intelligence.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** — OCI and Azure, Nutanix virtualization, migration,
  and high-availability / disaster-recovery operations.
- **Managed Services** — 24/7 operation of enterprise IT environments under SLA.
- **Training & Capacity Building** — technical training and knowledge transfer.

## Sectors Served

- **Government & Public Security** — Ministry of Interior Unified Operations
  Centers (911) in Riyadh, Dammam, and Madinah; integration and advanced digital
  solutions.
- **Regulatory** — Nuclear and Radiological Regulatory Commission: operation and
  maintenance of an integration-management platform.
- **Giga-projects** — NEOM (PIF-owned): connectivity, integration, and
  enterprise architecture for the public-security sector, and digital-services
  integration on Sindalah Island.
- **Real Estate / PIF Companies** — Real Estate Registry: development and
  operation of an API Gateway platform.
- **Higher Education** — Jouf University: 36-month IT managed-services program
  across a hybrid, multi-site environment.

- Flexible, scalable solutions designed to grow without re-engineering.
