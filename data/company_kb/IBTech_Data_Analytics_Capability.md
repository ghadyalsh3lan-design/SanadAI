# IBTech — Data Engineering & Analytics Capability Statement

## Overview

IBTech designs and operates modern data platforms that turn fragmented
operational data into governed, analytics-ready assets. Our data engineering
practice has delivered integration platforms, data pipelines, and analytics
solutions for government entities and Public Investment Fund (PIF) companies,
with particular depth in systems integration and API-led architectures.

## Core Competencies

- **Data integration and API platforms** — design and operation of enterprise
  integration layers and API gateways that connect heterogeneous systems.
- **Data pipelines (ETL/ELT)** — batch and streaming ingestion, transformation,
  and orchestration with Apache Airflow and Apache Spark.
- **Data warehousing and lakehouse** — dimensional modelling and lakehouse
  architectures for unified analytical storage.
- **Business intelligence and dashboards** — operational and executive
  dashboards, KPI tracking, and SLA reporting using Microsoft Power BI.
- **Data governance** — data cataloguing, lineage, access control, and quality
  monitoring aligned with national data protection requirements.

## Representative Engagements

- **Real Estate Registry (PIF-owned).** Developed and operated an **API Gateway
  platform**, exposing and governing registry services to consuming systems with
  monitoring, throttling, and security controls.
- **NEOM (PIF-owned).** Delivered connectivity, integration, and enterprise
  architecture for the public security sector, integrating digital services
  across a multi-system environment.
- **Ministry of Interior — Unified Operations Centers (911).** Built the
  integration and advanced digital-solutions layer feeding consolidated
  operational dashboards and incident trend analysis.
- **Nuclear and Radiological Regulatory Commission.** Operated and maintained an
  integration-management platform under a managed-services model.

## Technology Stack

Apache Airflow, Apache Spark, Python, SQL, Oracle Database, REST/API gateways,
Microsoft Power BI, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), and Microsoft Azure.

## Standards and Governance

Data engagements are delivered under the firm's ITIL-aligned operations model
and PMI-based project governance, with SLA compliance tracking, availability
measurement, and continuous-improvement reporting.
