# Nimbus Data Solutions — Key Team

Nimbus is led by an experienced team that combines technical depth with sector knowledge
across government, banking, healthcare, and telecommunications. The four leaders below
form the executive team, supported by a broader delivery organization.

## Layla Al-Harbi — Chief Executive Officer

Co-founder and CEO. 18 years of experience in data and technology consulting.
Holds an MBA from INSEAD and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).
Before founding Nimbus, Layla led the analytics function at a regional bank, where she
built the data team from scratch. As CEO she owns the company's responsible-AI commitments
and chairs the quarterly delivery-quality review.

## Omar Khan — Chief Technology Officer

Leads engineering and architecture. 15 years of experience building cloud data
platforms. AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional. Omar sets the company's
technical standards — infrastructure as code, version-controlled pipelines, and mandatory
peer review — and personally reviews the architecture of every major engagement before
delivery begins.

## Dr. Sara Mansour — Head of AI

Leads the Generative AI Center of Excellence. PhD in Machine Learning from KAUST,
with 11 years of experience and 9 peer-reviewed publications. Sara established the
company's model-evaluation practice, under which every model ships with an accuracy report
and a statement of limitations. She also leads the team's work on retrieval-augmented
assistants grounded in client documents.

## Yusuf Ali — Director of Delivery

Oversees project delivery and the PMO. 13 years of experience, certified Scrum
Master (CSM) and PMP. Has delivered more than 40 enterprise data projects. Yusuf is
responsible for the Agile delivery methodology used on every engagement and for the formal
handover process that closes each project.

## Delivery organization

Beneath the executive team sit cross-functional squads, each combining data engineers,
data scientists, and a delivery lead. Squads are deliberately kept small so that
accountability is clear, and senior architects rotate across squads to keep standards
consistent.

## Staffing

Nimbus has 240 employees, of whom 160 are technical delivery staff (engineers, data
scientists, and architects) and 18 work in the Generative AI Center of Excellence. The
remaining staff cover sales, operations, finance, and the project-management office.
Technical staff are encouraged to maintain at least one current cloud or data certification,
and the company funds training and exam costs.
