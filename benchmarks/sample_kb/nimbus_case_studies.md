# Nimbus Data Solutions — Case Studies

The following engagements illustrate the kind of measurable outcomes Nimbus delivers
across its priority sectors. Each project was delivered using the company's standard
Agile methodology and concluded with a formal handover and documentation package.

## Case study 1: National Health Data Platform

- Client: Ministry of Health (Saudi Arabia)
- Year: 2022
- Scope: Built a centralized data lake consolidating 14 hospital systems.
- Outcome: Reduced cross-hospital reporting time from 9 days to under 4 hours, and
  enabled a single national dashboard used by 1,200 staff.

Before the project, each of the 14 hospitals reported through its own systems and formats,
so producing a national picture meant weeks of manual consolidation. Nimbus designed a
governed data lake on AWS, built ingestion pipelines for every source system, and applied
consistent data-quality rules so that figures matched across hospitals. A medallion
architecture separated raw, cleansed, and reporting-ready data. The resulting national
dashboard gave the Ministry near-real-time visibility for the first time, and the platform
was handed over with full documentation and trained Ministry staff. This engagement is the
reason healthcare has become one of the company's fastest-growing sectors.

## Case study 2: Retail Bank Fraud Detection

- Client: Gulf Commercial Bank
- Year: 2023
- Scope: Delivered a real-time machine-learning fraud detection pipeline on Databricks.
- Outcome: Detected 38% more fraudulent transactions while cutting false positives by
  22%, saving an estimated 47 million SAR in the first year.

Gulf Commercial Bank's previous rules-based system flagged too many legitimate transactions
while still missing organized fraud. Nimbus built a streaming feature pipeline on Databricks
and trained gradient-boosted models that scored transactions in real time. Careful threshold
tuning and human-in-the-loop review reduced false positives even as detection improved. The
38% increase in detected fraud and 22% reduction in false positives translated into an
estimated 47 million SAR saved in the first year, and the model is retrained monthly under a
managed-operations agreement.

## Case study 3: Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

- Client: Etihad Telecom
- Year: 2021
- Scope: Developed a churn-prediction model and retention recommendation engine.
- Outcome: Improved churn prediction accuracy to 86% and reduced monthly churn by 1.9
  percentage points.

Etihad Telecom wanted to act before customers left rather than after. Nimbus combined
usage, billing, and support-ticket data to train a churn-prediction model that reached 86%
accuracy, and paired it with a recommendation engine that suggested the most effective
retention offer for each at-risk customer. Acting on these recommendations reduced monthly
churn by 1.9 percentage points, a large effect at the scale of a national telecom operator.

## What these projects have in common

Across all three engagements, the same pattern holds: consolidate trustworthy data, build a
reproducible model or platform on top of it, keep a human in the loop for high-stakes
decisions, and hand over complete documentation. This consistency is what lets Nimbus quote
measurable outcomes with confidence.
