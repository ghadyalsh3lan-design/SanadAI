# Nimbus Data Solutions — Policies and Service Standards

This document summarizes the policies and service standards that govern every Nimbus
engagement, from information security through to project delivery and data retention.

## Information security

Nimbus operates an ISO 27001-certified information security management system. All
client data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access follows
the principle of least privilege and is reviewed quarterly. Staff complete security
awareness training on joining and annually thereafter, and all production access is
logged and monitored. Security incidents are managed through a documented response plan
that includes client notification within agreed timeframes.

## Service level agreements

Managed-operations clients are covered by formal service-level agreements with the
following targets:

- Platform availability target: 99.9% uptime per calendar month.
- Critical (P1) incident response time: 30 minutes.
- High (P2) incident response time: 2 hours.
- Standard support hours: Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Arabia Standard Time).
- 24/7 on-call coverage is provided for P1 incidents.

Availability is measured per service and reported monthly. If a target is missed, the
monthly operations review documents the root cause and the corrective actions taken.
Service credits apply where contractually agreed.

## Delivery methodology

Nimbus delivers projects using an Agile (Scrum) methodology with two-week sprints,
governed by a central Project Management Office (PMO). Every engagement includes a
fixed discovery phase, iterative delivery, and a formal handover with documentation.
During discovery the team agrees the scope, success metrics, and architecture. Iterative
delivery proceeds in two-week sprints, each ending with a demo and a prioritized backlog
review. The PMO tracks scope, risk, and budget across all active engagements and escalates
issues early. The formal handover includes runbooks, architecture documentation, and
knowledge-transfer sessions so the client can operate the solution independently.

## Quality management

Quality is governed by an ISO 9001-certified quality-management system. Code is peer
reviewed, pipelines are tested, and every deliverable is reproducible from version control.

## Data retention

Client data is retained only for the duration of the engagement and securely deleted
within 30 days of contract closure, unless a longer period is contractually required.
Backups follow the same retention rules, and deletion is certified to the client on
request.
