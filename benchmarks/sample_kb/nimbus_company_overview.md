# Nimbus Data Solutions — Company Overview

Nimbus Data Solutions was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
The company employs 240 people across three offices: Riyadh, Dubai, and Cairo.

## History

Nimbus Data Solutions began in 2017 as a four-person data consultancy working out of a
shared office in Riyadh. The founders had previously led analytics teams inside large
government and banking organizations and saw that most enterprises in the region were
struggling to move beyond spreadsheets and isolated reports. The first engagements were
small data-warehouse projects for local financial firms. By 2019 the company had grown to
roughly 60 staff and opened its Dubai office to serve clients across the United Arab
Emirates. In 2021 Nimbus opened a third office in Cairo, which today hosts much of the
company's data-engineering and managed-operations talent. Growth has been organic and
profitable every year since 2018, funded entirely from operations rather than outside
investment.

## Mission

Nimbus helps public-sector and enterprise clients modernize their data platforms and
adopt artificial intelligence responsibly. Our mission is to turn fragmented data into
trusted, decision-ready insight. We believe that data products should be reproducible,
well documented, and owned by the client at the end of every engagement — never locked
behind a vendor. Responsible adoption of AI is central to the mission: every model
Nimbus ships is accompanied by an evaluation report, a description of its limitations,
and a plan for human oversight.

## Sectors served

Nimbus delivers projects across four primary sectors:

- Government and public services
- Healthcare
- Banking and financial services
- Telecommunications

Government and public services is the largest sector by revenue, reflecting the company's
roots in national data programs. Healthcare has grown quickly since 2022 on the back of
hospital-data-platform work. Banking and financial services engagements tend to be the
most technically demanding, often involving real-time fraud and risk systems, while
telecommunications projects center on customer analytics and churn reduction.

## Mission in practice

Every Nimbus engagement is structured to leave the client more capable than before.
Knowledge transfer, paired delivery with client staff, and complete documentation are
contractual deliverables, not afterthoughts. This is how Nimbus reconciles fast delivery
with long-term client ownership.

## Ownership and leadership

Nimbus is privately held. The company is led by its Chief Executive Officer, Layla Al-Harbi,
who co-founded the firm in 2017. The leadership team combines deep technical expertise with
sector experience in government, banking, healthcare, and telecommunications, and the board
meets quarterly to review delivery quality, client satisfaction, and the company's
responsible-AI commitments.
