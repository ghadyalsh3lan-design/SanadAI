What does the RFP actually say?
Answer

The RFP explicitly outlines the following key points regarding communication and proponent responsibilities:

1. Review and reporting errors: Proponents must promptly review all RFP documents and report any errors, omissions, or ambiguities in writing to the designated RFP Contact by the Deadline for Questions.

2. No post-deadline responses: The Issuer (IWK) is not obligated to address questions submitted after the Deadline for Questions, nor to acknowledge addenda issued beyond that deadline.

3. Estimates and responsibility: All quantities, data, or opinions in the RFP — including those in addenda — are estimates only. Proponents must independently gather all information needed to prepare a compliant proposal.

4. Self-reliance on deliverables: Proponents bear full responsibility for verifying their ability to meet the deliverables and pricing in the Pricing Form (Appendix C).
